# Backlink and authority plan

Date: 9 November 2026
Current verified backlinks: none measured. No backlink tool was used, so this plan describes actions,
not existing links.

## Principle

For a small Ghanaian software company, authority comes from being cited by institutions and
practitioners that already have trust. Buy links, exchange links in bulk or publish guest posts on
generic "write for us" sites would add risk without adding relevance. The plan below is slow,
manual and verifiable.

## Priority order

### 1. Company and founder profiles (foundation)

Owned profiles that search engines can verify:

- Google Business Profile, if the business qualifies and a real service area is used. Do not invent
  a storefront.
- LinkedIn company page and founder profile.
- GitHub organisation and repository links where the code is public.
- Any professional body or alumni network the founder genuinely belongs to.

Use the same name, URL and description everywhere. Do not create profiles on sites that require a
fake address or a purchased listing.

### 2. Product listings and directories

Where a product is released, submit it to directories that accept real products and do not charge
for a "dofollow" review:

- Product Hunt at launch, once, with an honest description.
- Relevant education technology directories for SchemeKnit.
- Ghanaian startup and SME directories that verify listings.
- Free software directories that allow a company profile.

TillProof is not listed until there is a released application.

### 3. Practitioner and institutional citation

This is the highest-value category and the slowest:

- Teacher networks, subject associations and continuing professional development providers for
  SchemeKnit.
- Mobile money agent associations, susu groups and small business associations for MaaCash.
- Electrical dealers' associations and trade groups for TillProof.
- Ghanaian universities and polytechnics where the founder can give a guest session or supervise a
  project.

Approach with a useful artefact, not a request. A free workshop, a printed checklist from an
existing article, or a worked example is more likely to earn a citation than an email asking for a
link.

### 4. Local media and industry press

Pitch a story only when there is a real event: a product launch, a partnership, a published piece of
research, a milestone. Do not pitch "our new website". Ghanaian technology and business outlets are
more likely to cover a concrete local problem than a generic software company.

### 5. Partner and supplier pages

If the company works with a recognised partner, ask whether the partner lists suppliers or projects.
This applies to hosting, payment, integration and implementation partners. No reciprocal link
requirement should be accepted if it creates a link scheme.

## Content that attracts links

The three resource articles are written to be citable:

- A method for turning a scheme of learning into lesson plans.
- A daily recordkeeping routine for mobile money businesses.
- A stock-control method for electrical shops.

Each is specific enough to be referenced by a trainer, an association or another practitioner. That
is the intended link asset, not a landing page.

Future linkable assets, in priority order:

1. A printable one-page opening and closing checklist for mobile money agents.
2. A printable weekly planning template for teachers.
3. A cycle-count schedule template for a small electrical shop.
4. A short, honest guide to software procurement for Ghanaian SMEs.

All should remain ungated. A download that requires an email address earns fewer citations.

## What not to do

- No paid link packages, Fiverr link building or "100 backlinks" offers.
- No private blog networks.
- No mass guest posting on unrelated sites.
- No link exchanges with competitors or unrelated directories.
- No fake reviews, awards, memberships or "featured in" logos.
- No directory submissions that require inventing an address.

## Measurement

Track only what can be observed:

| Metric | Source | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified referring domains | Search Console Links report | Monthly |
| New mentions without links | Manual search / alerts | Monthly |
| Links from priority categories | Manual log | Monthly |
| Referral sessions | GA4, if analytics is enabled and consented | Monthly |

Keep a simple log with: date, source URL, target URL, anchor text, category, whether it was
editorial or self-created. Do not estimate domain authority from an unverified tool and present it as
fact.

## 90-day target

Realistic target for the first 90 days:

- 5–10 legitimate, relevant referring domains.
- At least two from practitioner or institutional sources.
- Zero paid or exchanged links.
- Every profile consistent with `https://bloomcoretech.com`.

If fewer links arrive but the profiles and two strong citations exist, that is a better outcome than
twenty low-quality directory links.
