# Keyword strategy and page mapping

Date: 9 November 2026
Market: Ghana, English-language search.
Method: qualitative review of live search results and the language used by Ghanaian institutions,
vendors and practitioners. No search volume, difficulty, CPC or ranking data was available, so none
is claimed. Validate demand in Search Console after indexing before committing to a large content
programme.

## Principles

1. One primary intent per URL. A page may support several secondary queries but must answer one
   question better than any other page on the site.
2. Write for how the work is described locally: "scheme of learning", "susu", "mobile money agent",
   "electrical shop", not imported enterprise vocabulary.
3. Do not target queries the company cannot honestly satisfy. A product still in development does
   not target "buy" or "pricing" intent.
4. Company-name and product-name queries are the highest-converting cluster and must stay clean.

## Clusters

### Cluster A — Company and services (commercial)

Primary URLs: `/`, `/services`, `/about`, `/work`, `/contact`.

Language to own: "custom software", "web application", "data systems", "process digitisation",
"API integration", "Ghanaian organisations". Avoid competing on generic global terms such as
"software development company" without a country or sector modifier; the site is too small to win
those and the traffic would not be local.

### Cluster B — Education technology (product + informational)

Primary URL: `/products/schemeknit`. Supporting URL: the scheme-of-learning article.

Language to own: "scheme of learning", "lesson plan", "learning indicator", "content standard",
"strand and sub-strand", "NaCCA". The article explains the method; the product page offers the tool.
Never claim NaCCA endorsement, accreditation or official status.

### Cluster C — Financial and business operations (product + informational)

Primary URL: `/products/maacash`. Supporting URL: the mobile money recordkeeping article.

Language to own: "mobile money agent", "float", "susu collection", "reconciliation", "multi-location
operations", "role-based access". State clearly that MaaCash is an operations platform, not a bank,
a mobile money provider or a deposit-taking institution.

### Cluster D — Retail and inventory (product + informational)

Primary URL: `/products/tillproof`. Supporting URL: the electrical shop stock control article.

Language to own: "electrical shop", "stock control", "item code", "cycle count", "price control",
"cash accountability". This cluster can earn informational traffic while the product is unfinished.
Do not target purchase intent until there is a released application.

## Title and description policy

- Titles state the page's subject plus a concrete qualifier, and stay within roughly 50–60
  characters where possible.
- Descriptions state what the reader gets, in one sentence, and never repeat the title verbatim.
- No keyword stuffing, no "best", "number one", "leading" or invented superlatives.
- Home title leads with the company name, then "Software Development in Ghana".
- Product titles lead with the product name, then the category the buyer would use.

## Internal linking rules

1. Every product page links to the other two products.
2. Every resource article links to its related product and to `/resources`.
3. The home page links to all three products and to `/resources`.
4. The header exposes Products, Services, Work and Resources.
5. Anchor text describes the destination. No "click here".

## What to measure after indexing

Without a volume source, use Search Console as the research instrument:

- Queries that already produce impressions with a position worse than 20 are expansion candidates.
- Queries with impressions but very low CTR are title/description candidates.
- Pages with no impressions after four weeks need either a link, an internal link, or better intent
  match.
- Only then decide whether to add a new article, and write it because the question is real.

## Explicit non-targets

- No location pages for cities the company does not serve from.
- No "best software company in Ghana" listicles.
- No competitor-name comparison pages without verified, current facts.
- No pricing pages for products whose pricing is not published.
- No NaCCA, GES, Bank of Ghana or regulator endorsement claims.
